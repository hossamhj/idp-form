Q1 — Foundation Months 1–3

The Coordinator learns systems, builds habits, and starts executing under guidance.

🏢
AGM — your actions this quarter
What you must do to make Q1 work

Q1 is your heaviest investment. The daily check-in takes 10 minutes but prevents failure at month 12. Every action below is your responsibility — not HR's, not the Buddy's.

1
Week 1: Assign and brief the Buddy. Tell them exactly what you expect — daily 10-minute check-in, one written observation per day.
2
Week 1: Book the first Monthly IDP session (end of month 1). Book all 3 quarterly IDP sessions now.
3
Week 2: Confirm the Coordinator has started all 4 formal training modules. Follow up on completion.
4
Month 1: Identify the first "mini ownership" moment — one specific task the Coordinator will own independently for the first time. Plan it deliberately.
5
Monthly: Review Buddy's observation log. Give written feedback on at least one entry per week.
6
End of Q1: Rate all competencies below. Add at least 3 evidence entries. Complete the Q1 decision and sign.
⭐
GM — your role in Q1
What you need to verify and enable

Your job in Q1 is to make sure the AGM is doing theirs. Development doesn't happen without deliberate manager investment.

1
Month 1: In your monthly AGM meeting, confirm: Buddy assigned? IDP sessions booked? Training started?
2
Month 2: Ask the AGM: what is the Coordinator's first "own it" moment? If they can't answer — intervene.
3
End of Q1: Review the AGM's Q1 ratings and evidence log. If fewer than 3 evidence entries exist — the AGM has not done their job.
⚠️
Warning signals — act immediately if you see these
Don't wait for the quarter to end
!
Buddy is not doing daily check-ins by week 2 → Replace the Buddy or take over yourself.
!
Training not completed by end of month 1 → Block time and complete it with them.
!
Coordinator is still escalating everything by month 2 → The first ownership moment hasn't been assigned. Do it now.
!
No evidence logged after 6 weeks → The AGM is not creating development opportunities. GM must intervene.

Q2 — Building Confidence Months 4–6

The Coordinator works with greater autonomy — owns interactions, leads walkthroughs independently.

🏢
AGM — your actions this quarter
Q2: step back deliberately — but stay close

Q2 is about giving the Coordinator real ownership — and watching honestly what happens. Resist the urge to jump in before they've had a chance to handle it.

1
Month 4: Brief the Buddy: step back to weekly debrief only. The Coordinator owns their interactions now.
2
Month 4: Assign first solo Arrival — brief them before, debrief them after. Write a specific observation.
3
Month 5: Let them handle the first real complaint independently. Do not intervene unless they are about to make a serious mistake.
4
Monthly: Hold IDP sessions — review what they owned, what they escalated, and whether the escalations were appropriate.
5
End of Q2: Rate all competencies. Minimum 3 new evidence entries this quarter. Complete decision and sign.
⭐
GM — your role in Q2
Mid-point check — is the AGM giving real ownership?
1
Month 4: Ask the AGM: what specific ownership has been given this quarter? If the answer is vague — push for specifics.
2
Month 5: Conduct a brief Coffee Chat with the Coordinator — informally assess how they're feeling and what they're learning.
3
End of Q2: Review AGM's evidence log and ratings. If the Coordinator is not advancing — decide: extension or fundamental review.

Q3 — Proving Ownership Months 7–9

The Coordinator handles complexity, guides newer team members, and leads community moments.

🏢
AGM — your actions this quarter
Q3: assign real challenges — not safe ones

Q3 is where you find out if they are truly ready. Give them situations that are at the edge of their current capability — and observe honestly.

1
Month 7: Assign the Coordinator to own a zone or floor independently for a full shift. Debrief in detail.
2
Month 8: Let them guide a newer team member on at least one task — observe how they explain and support.
3
Month 8–9: Plan and debrief one community activation they lead end-to-end.
4
Month 9: Cover a full Associate shift independently — you are not available. Debrief the next day.
5
End of Q3: Honest mid-point review. If they are not at ≥ 3 on most competencies — do not let this reach Q4 unprepared.
⭐
GM — your role in Q3
Is this person truly performing at Associate level?
1
Month 8: Ask the AGM directly: "Is this person ready for promotion in one more quarter?" If the answer is uncertain — discuss now.
2
End of Q3: Review evidence log — are entries specific and substantive? If they are vague, the AGM is not observing properly.
3
End of Q3: If ratings are below 3 on more than 2 competencies — an extension or path review is needed before Q4.

Q4 — Promotion Gate Months 10–12

Final assessment. All criteria must be met and documented before the promotion is approved.

🏢
AGM — your actions in Q4
Build the evidence file and make the case

If you have done Q1–Q3 well, Q4 should be confirmation — not discovery. The promotion file is built from everything you've documented, not from what you remember.

1
Month 10–11: Observe consistently performing at Associate level — not just occasionally. Document every instance.
2
Month 11: Request the Buddy's final written rating and observation.
3
Month 12: Complete all final competency ratings. Calculate the readiness score. Prepare the written promotion recommendation.
4
Month 12: Submit the full evidence file to the GM — not a summary, the full file with all quarterly ratings and evidence entries.
⭐
GM — promotion approval
Review the evidence — then decide
1
Month 11: Conduct a Coffee Chat with the Coordinator — 15 minutes. You are assessing leadership maturity, not testing knowledge.
2
Month 12: Review the full evidence file — not just the final ratings. Are there enough evidence entries? Are they substantive?
3
Month 12: If the score is ≥ 3.5 and the evidence file is complete — approve promotion and notify HR formally.
4
If you are not satisfied with the evidence — do not approve. Ask the AGM to extend and add more documented evidence before resubmitting.
