The shift this transition is really about
59% of your work today is assisting others. The move to Associate is not mainly a skills change — it is a change in how you measure yourself.
"I succeeded because I helped" → "I succeeded because I delivered"
A skill is built by practice. A value only changes through a deliberate conversation — and someone whose value has not changed reverts under the first real pressure.
Three functions in focus
Deliberate development runs 2–4 functions at a time. Presenting all 26 as active work is what turns a development plan into a form.
26 functions change — but they are not treated alike
Split by what actually needs deliberate practice.
Self first, then manager
Rate yourself before seeing Raghdah's rating. The conversation starts from the gap, not the score — and the gap itself is the development material. Consistent is the gate standard; the real test is always under pressure.
Learning agility
The strongest predictor of success at the next level is not how well you do today's job — it is how fast you learn a new one. Recorded once, on Shift handover, your first entirely new function.
Five gates — all of them, no averaging
Strong on one and weak on another is a specific, actionable finding. An averaged score hides exactly what the promotion decision turns on.
