What makes it hold

Four safeguards — and one warning sign

1
No promotion without a gate record
Five checks, all of them, no averaging. A decision with no record is not one the system accepts.
2
Quarterly calibration
Every coach rates the same case; the spread is discussed. Without it, "consistent" means something different in each property within a year.
3
Reflection is never cited against you
The practice log records mistakes. Anyone may read it; no one may quote it in a decision — otherwise people write for the audience.
4
Criteria published before they apply
Nobody is measured against a standard they have not seen, and everyone reads everything written about them.
!
The risk here is growth, not favouritism
Promotion means taking a new building, so a coach whose people advance has expanded the company — the incentive points the right way. The danger is a building opening with no one ready, and the bar quietly moving to fill the seat.

A gate pass rate that rises during rapid expansion is a warning, not good news.
