Flow MENA — Building Operations  ·  Skill-Based & Role-Based Progression

The core shift

Coordinator thinks
"Did I complete my tasks and respond to what came my way?"
→
Associate thinks
"Is the Neighbour's experience seamless — and is the building running the way it should?"
The gap in one sentence: The Coordinator executes assigned tasks with warmth and reliability. The Associate owns the Neighbour experience end-to-end — resolving issues, building trust, and keeping the building at Flow's standards without being directed to do so.

What needs to develop

Neighbour Ownership
Move from responding to requests to fully owning the Neighbour relationship — proactively anticipating needs, resolving issues without escalation, and building the kind of trust that makes residents feel genuinely at home.
Operational Depth
Move from monitoring and reporting building issues to actively ensuring buildings are in top condition — identifying issues early, following through to resolution, and keeping all spaces consistently ready without being prompted.
Independent Judgement
Move from escalating most situations to exercising confident field judgement — knowing what to resolve independently, what to escalate, and how to handle the messy middle without waiting for direction.
Community Presence
Move from supporting community activities to actively bringing community moments to life — engaging neighbours, creating atmosphere, and helping build a connected residential community.

Development programme — 70-20-10

70%
On-the-job experience
  • Own a Neighbour issue from first contact to full resolution — no escalation
  • Lead a full building walkthrough and follow through on every item found
  • Be the primary point of contact for a designated floor or zone independently
  • Handle a difficult Neighbour situation — complaint, frustration, or conflict
  • Bring a community moment to life — plan and run a small activation
  • Cover an Associate shift fully and independently
  • Complete a full operational check — spaces, amenities, maintenance log
20%
Mentoring & coaching
  • Buddy: experienced RX Associate — daily debrief in Phase 1
  • Shadow Associate during complex Neighbour situations
  • Weekly debrief with Senior RX Associate or RX Manager
  • Written reflection after every challenging situation handled
  • Monthly IDP session with AGM — review evidence, set next milestone
10%
Formal training
  • HR Policies — Flow MENA (internal)
  • Flow Brand Standards (internal)
  • Resident Experience SOPs (internal)
  • Flow Technology — systems & tools (internal)
  • Service Recovery & Difficult Conversations (internal)

Development phases

Phase 1 · Foundation
  • Complete all formal training
  • Shadow Associate on every interaction type
  • Handle first-line requests independently
  • Daily debrief with Buddy
  • Building walkthrough under supervision
Phase 2 · Building Confidence
  • Own Neighbour interactions solo
  • Lead building walkthroughs independently
  • Handle first complaint end-to-end
  • Buddy steps back — weekly debrief only
  • Support first community activation
Phase 3 · Proving Ownership
  • Own a zone or floor independently
  • Handle difficult situations without escalation
  • Lead a community moment end-to-end
  • Cover Associate shift solo
  • Mid-point assessment with AGM
Phase 4 · Promotion Ready
  • Consistently performing at Associate level
  • Zero escalations that should have been resolved
  • Building stays at standard across full shifts
  • AGM evidence file complete
  • Final promotion assessment

AGM and GM responsibilities

AGM's role
  • Assigns first "own it" moment — monitors and debriefs
  • Selects and assesses Buddy readiness carefully
  • Holds monthly IDP session — reviews evidence, not opinions
  • Identifies real situations that build the 70%
  • Conducts phase assessments and documents decisions
  • Builds and submits the promotion evidence file to GM
GM's role
  • Reviews development plan in monthly AGM meeting
  • Ensures AGM is creating real development experiences
  • Conducts one Coffee Chat before final promotion gate
  • Reviews evidence file and formally approves promotion

Promotion gate — readiness criteria

All criteria must be met and documented before the formal promotion is approved
Neighbour ownership demonstrated — consistent Building at standard across full shifts Difficult situations handled without escalation Community moment led independently Associate shift covered solo Buddy rating ≥ 4/5 AGM rating ≥ 4/5 All 4 phase checklists complete

What happens if criteria are not met?

All criteria met
Promoted to RX Associate — AGM notifies HR formally
→
Partially met
Extended review period + written improvement plan from AGM
→
Not met
Fundamental review with AGM and GM — is this the right path?
